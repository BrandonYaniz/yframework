# Milestone 12: Localization

## Governing contract

Localization is persistent derived content, not repeated request-time translation. Components own localizable resource definitions, stable localization units, source extraction/context, prompts, validation, source eligibility, localized domain tables/files, and final persistence/read behavior. The framework owns coordination, source revision/fingerprint comparison, preferred/adaptive policy, deduplication, idle-only proactive scheduling, on-demand generation, review-required state, operational localization messages, LLM submission, estimates, and administrative coordination. Supported locales may be manually or adaptively preferred; effective preferred is their union. V1 may promote by meaningful aggregate demand and never automatically demotes. Preferred locales proactively backfill through idle-only work, supported non-preferred locales generate on first demand. Only changed units regenerate. Human-modified or locked stale units become review_required and are never silently overwritten. Failed requested localization does not silently fall back to primary-language content by default. Completion notifications occur only after localized domain data commits.
